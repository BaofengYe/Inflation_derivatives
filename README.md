# Inflation Derivatives

Research notes on the pricing and modelling of inflation-linked derivatives.

## Contents

- [**Assessing Seasonality in Inflation-Linked Derivatives**](./assessing-seasonality-in-inflation-linked-derivatives.md) — a framework for detecting, estimating, and pricing-consistently modelling the seasonal component of consumer price indices (CPI-U, HICPxT, RPI). Covers zero-coupon and year-on-year inflation swaps, inflation-linked bonds, seasonal unit-root testing (HEGY, Canova–Hansen), estimation models (X-13ARIMA-SEATS, TRAMO-SEATS, STL, structural/state-space, SARIMA, machine-learning), pricing-consistent curve construction, the COVID-era structural break, market-implied seasonality, and model-risk quantification.
